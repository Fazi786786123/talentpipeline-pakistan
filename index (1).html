Employer trust & verification architecture

The Verification Standard every listed candidate passes

Foreign employers should not have to take a CV on faith. Three independent checks run before a profile can go live, and each one leaves a record the employer can read.

Background verification

Who the candidate is, and what they studied, confirmed against the source.

  • National ID matched against a live selfie check
  • Degree confirmed directly with the issuing university
  • Two professional or academic references contacted
  • Public profile ownership proven by a signed commit

Code quality audit

Senior engineers review the work itself, not a certificate about the work.

  • Commit history checked for authorship and consistency
  • Structure, tests, security basics and readability scored out of 100
  • Live deployment opened and exercised by a reviewer
  • A minimum score of 75 required to list

Secure legal contracts

The agreement is between the employer and the candidate, with nobody in the middle.

  • Employer company registration and identity checked first
  • Standard contract template reviewed by counsel in both countries
  • Pay terms, notice period and IP ownership in writing
  • Candidate keeps all original documents

Questions people ask first

Do candidates ever pay anything?

No. Candidates pay no registration, training, placement or document fee. The platform is funded by employer subscriptions. Anyone asking a candidate for money in our name should be reported.

Who signs the employment contract?

The employer and the candidate sign directly. TalentPipeline supplies a reviewed template and verifies both parties, but it is not the employer and does not take a cut of salary.

How is code quality judged?

Senior reviewers score each portfolio out of 100 on structure, tests, security basics and readability, and they open the live deployment. A score of 75 or more is needed to appear in the marketplace.

What happens to my documents?

You keep your originals. Verification uses copies and direct checks with the issuing institution. We never hold a passport or original degree.

Can employers hire for remote work only?

Yes. Remote contracts, relocation with employer-sponsored visas, and contract-to-hire are all supported. Visa sponsorship is always the employer's responsibility and is never charged to the candidate.
